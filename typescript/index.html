(About the college)

Part of the KRS Group of Institutions, whose roots go back to the Indian Institute of Technical Education in 1947. Founded by Prof. K. R. Sundararajan, MSEC began in 2001 with three branches and has grown into a campus for seven, built on one idea: engineering is learned by building.
